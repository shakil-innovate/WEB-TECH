About PayBridge

PayBridge was founded to solve a real problem — millions of people in developing countries cannot access international payment platforms due to limited banking infrastructure.

We bridge that gap by connecting users with trusted payment agents who have international cards and can complete transactions on their behalf.

Every transaction is tracked, every conversation is logged, and every agent is verified by our super admin team to ensure your money is always safe.
